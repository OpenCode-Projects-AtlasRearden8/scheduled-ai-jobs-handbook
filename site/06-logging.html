06 · Logging: knowing what actually happened

A scheduled job you can't inspect is a rumor. Logging is how the rumor becomes a record — and it matters far more for scheduled AI jobs than for ordinary ones, because the output is text nobody wanted to check before it mattered.

The scripts in this repo all follow the same log discipline. Here's what it is and why.

The one-line-per-run log

Every job writes one line per event to logs/<job-name>.log:

2026-11-07T06:47:02Z [NOTE] lock acquired
2026-11-07T06:47:02Z [START] job 'morning-digest' started from notes.md (1240 chars)
2026-11-07T06:47:09Z [DONE] success; prompt 1513 chars, reply 708 chars -> morning-digest-2026-11-07.md

Rotating: no log grows forever

Left alone, a log a misbehaving job spams is a runaway job wearing a hat. ai_job.py rotates a log past 5 MB into a timestamped side file before appending — plus the watchdog (Chapter 9) reports and prunes oversized logs on a schedule:

python3 scripts/watchdog.py --job-dir logs           # report only
python3 scripts/watchdog.py --job-dir logs --prune   # clean oversized

Log math worth knowing: a 100-line/month log will never rotate. A job writing 5 lines per retry attempt chews through 5 MB in a few weeks. Rotation isn't bureaucracy; it's the alarm bell itself.

From cron's mouth vs the job's mouth

Two logs are better than one:

happened inside. Written by the job itself.

  • The job log (logs/<job>.log) is the content — did it run, what
  • The scheduler log is the heartbeat — did the scheduler even fire it.

Chapter 3 shows the scheduler-side line:

0 7 * * *  $HOME/scheduled-ai-jobs/jobs/daily-digest.sh >> $HOME/scheduled-ai-jobs/logs/cron.log 2>&1

The diagnosis flows naturally:

SymptomSuspect
Nothing in cron.log, nothing in job logThe scheduler didn't fire the job at all
cron.log has output, job log doesn'tThe job crashed before writing a log line (wrong path to python, etc.)
Job log shows START but no DONEThe job died mid-run (timeout, machine shut down)
Job log shows [FAILED] with HTTP errorsEndpoint/model trouble
Job log shows [DONE] but output looks wrongA bad-output day (quality — your eyes)

START-with-no-DONE is worth its own emphasis: it's the log entry for "a job was taken halfway" — exactly what a stale lock in Chapter 9 catches.
